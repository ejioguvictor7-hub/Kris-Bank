About Kris Bank

A bank built around a table, not a call centre.

Kris Bank started with a simple complaint: banking in Lagos had gotten too big to feel personal. So we built the opposite — one branch, one book, and a manager who actually knows your account.

How we're different

Three things we don't compromise on.

01

Local by design

Headquartered on Victoria Island, Lagos State — no call centres, no routing between departments.

02

Named relationship manager

Every account holder is assigned one manager who signs off on statements, requests, and rate reviews personally.

03

Plain-language statements

No jargon on your ledger. Every line is written the way we'd explain it to you across the table.

Our story

How Kris Bank came together.

A small timeline, told plainly.

Year one

Kris Bank House opens

The first (and still only) branch opens on Victoria Island, Lagos State, with a handful of savings and current accounts.

Year two

Fixed deposits introduced

Members ask for a way to lock in savings for a fixed term — 3, 6, and 12 month deposit accounts are added to the ledger.

Year three

Private advisory begins

One-on-one appointments start for members planning around property, school fees, and long-term goals.

Today

Still one branch, on purpose

We've stayed small enough that every manager still knows every account by name.

What we believe

The principles behind the ledger.

Say it plainly

If a statement, fee, or rate can't be explained in one sentence, we don't offer it.

Stay reachable

Every member can call, email, or walk into the branch and speak to the person who manages their account — not a queue.

Grow slowly

We'd rather serve a smaller number of people well than a large number of people adequately.

Keep it in writing

Every rate we quote is agreed upfront and in writing — no changes without a conversation first.

Come say hello

Meet your manager in person.

Kris Bank House is open Monday to Friday on Victoria Island. Stop by or book a time that works for you.
